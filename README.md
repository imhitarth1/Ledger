# 💜 Ledger

### Your money. Your records. Your privacy.

Ledger is a simple, privacy-focused personal expense tracker that helps you manage income, track expenses, monitor your balance, and understand your spending habits.

Designed for everyday use, Ledger offers a clean interface, useful analytics, offline support, and local data storage without requiring an account or sign-in.

🌐 **[Open Ledger](https://ledger-expenses.netlify.app)**

---

## 📲 Install Ledger

Ledger is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on a supported device and use it like a regular app.

### 💻 On Windows or desktop

1. Open **[Ledger](https://ledger-expenses.netlify.app)** in a supported browser, such as Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2. Look for the **Install** icon in the browser's address bar.
3. Alternatively, open the browser menu and look for **Install Ledger** or **Install app**.
4. Click Install and open Ledger from your installed apps.

*The installation option may vary depending on your browser.*

### 📱 On Android

1. Open **[Ledger](https://ledger-expenses.netlify.app)** in Chrome or another supported browser.
2. Open the browser menu.
3. Select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, depending on the options available.
4. Follow the prompts to finish installation.

### 🍎 On iPhone or iPad

1. Open **[Ledger](https://ledger-expenses.netlify.app)** in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap Add.

Once installed, open Ledger from your device's home screen or app launcher.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Safety

### Your financial data stays on your device.

Ledger stores your transactions and settings in your browser's local storage. It does not require an account, and your financial records are not sent to a Ledger-operated backend or stored in a Ledger cloud database.

Here's what that means:

- **No account required:** Start using Ledger without signing up or logging in.
- **Local storage:** Your transactions stay in the browser profile where you created them.
- **No automatic cloud sync:** Your financial records are not automatically synchronized across devices.
- **Offline use:** Once the app files are cached, you can use supported features without an internet connection.
- **Your backups, your control:** Export and restore your records using the built-in JSON backup feature.

### ⚠️ Important: Protect your data

Local storage is convenient, but it is not a substitute for a backup.

- Clearing your browser's site data, resetting your browser, or losing access to your device may result in permanent data loss.
- Installing Ledger on another device does not automatically transfer your existing transactions.
- Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to access your saved records.
- JSON backup files contain your financial data. Store them somewhere secure and avoid sharing them publicly.
- The app is hosted on Netlify, which delivers the application files. This is separate from storing your transactions in a Ledger cloud database.

**Recommended:** Export a JSON backup regularly, especially after recording many transactions. Keep a copy somewhere safe, such as a secure cloud drive or USB drive.

## 📴 Using Ledger Offline

Ledger supports offline use through Progressive Web App technology.

For the best experience:

1. Open Ledger while connected to the internet.
2. Allow the application to load completely.
3. Install Ledger on your device if you wish.
4. Once its application files are cached, disconnect from the internet and try using it.

You can continue recording transactions offline when the app is available and its required files have been cached.

**Remember:** Offline transactions are stored locally on that device. They will not automatically appear on your other devices.
